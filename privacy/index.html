Privacy Policy

WallConnectorLog · last updated 1 September 2026

WallConnectorLog does not collect any data.

The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party SDKs. The developer receives nothing about you or your charger, because there is no server belonging to the developer for anything to be sent to.

What the app connects to

WallConnectorLog connects only to the address you enter yourself in Settings — your own WallConnectorLog server. There are no other connections: no third-party services, no map data, no update checks.

What is stored on your device

Your server address is stored in the app's own storage on your iPhone. It stays on the device, is included in your device backups, and is deleted when you delete the app.

Local network access

iOS asks for permission to reach devices on your local network. WallConnectorLog needs it for the ordinary case of a server running on your own network. It is used for nothing else, and the app does not scan the network.

Location

WallConnectorLog does not use your iPhone's location services.

Children

The app is not directed at children, and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with a new date.
